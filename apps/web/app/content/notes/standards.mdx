Writing `2026-10-09` instead of `10/09/26` isn’t about looks. It’s that nobody, and no machine, can misread it.

That’s what a standard is: a letter to people you will never meet. Maybe a classmate in another country, maybe you in three years, maybe a screen reader. Following it is being polite to them in advance.

This site tries to:

- Write every time in ISO 8601. The [Timestamps tool](/tools/time) also tells you which week it is (the ISO week, starting on Monday).
- Check color contrast against WCAG; the [Color tool](/tools/color) does the math.
- Use `<button>` for buttons, `<a>` for links and `<label>` for form fields, and respect the system’s “reduce motion” setting in animations.
- Use v7 for new UUIDs (RFC 9562), because they sort by time, which database indexes like.
